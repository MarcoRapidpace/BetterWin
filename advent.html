🎄 The Plan

The calendar itself is built: 24 doors, one a day, each open for 24 hours, with a Grand Prize revealed on 25 December. Doors show as Claimed, Expired, Today or Locked, and most rewards stay hidden as a Mystery Bonus until the day. The CRM job is to get players back to that page every day without burning the list.

No door-level events

The platform will not fire per-player events for doors claimed or expired, so every email here is date-driven and segment-driven. Nothing depends on knowing whether one player opened door 9.

The email reveals what the page hides

Because the tile says Mystery Bonus, the daily email is the only place to find out early what is behind today's door. That gives the daily send a real job instead of being a reminder.

Mystery makes tiering invisible

Nobody sees what anyone else got, so low value can get spins and VIP can get cashback behind the identical tile, with no fairness conversation.

Daily sending is defensible here and only here: across 1.4M campaigns, unsubscribe rates run 0.38% weekly, 0.33% twice weekly and 0.36% daily, so frequency is not the risk. Sending daily to people who never asked for a daily thing is.

📊 Cadence by Segment
SegmentDefinition in RapidpaceEmails, 24 Nov – 25 DecGets
EngagedOpened an email in the last 7 days, or last login within 3 days~28Daily reveal plus every milestone and big door
QuietNo open in 7 days, last login 4–30 days~11Weekly digest, two closing nudges, the four big doors
DormantLast login over 30 days4Launch, 12 Dec, 24 Dec, Grand Prize only
HoldoutRandom 10% of the eligible base0Nothing, for the whole month
Players move between segments automatically as they open or stop opening, so the cadence self-corrects. The holdout is the only way to say in January what the calendar actually added, since no door-level events exist to measure against.

⚠ Open Points
Does the Grand Prize need a minimum?

If it depends on opening a set number of doors, the milestone emails change shape and a streak-save email becomes worth building. If it is open to everyone, they stay as countdowns.

Are activations instant?

The 10 Dec tile shows Activate, which suggests instant credit. If any doors need a deposit before the reward lands, those days need their own copy and their own expectations.

Flow suppression

The deposit ladder, 3 Days Quiet and the weekly newsletter all need holding back for December, or they stack on top of the calendar.
